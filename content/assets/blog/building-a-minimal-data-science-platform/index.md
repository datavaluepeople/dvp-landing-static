At datavaluepeople we have set up the data science practice for a number of clients, giving us
hands-on experience establishing data science platforms across a variety of organisations and
tech stacks. Over time, we've developed clear opinions on how to do this well.

In October 2023, I gave a talk on the topic at the
[Data Science Festival OktoberFest 2023](https://datasciencefestival.com/session/building-a-minimal-data-science-platform/).

## The talk

Data science teams often support diverse projects across their organisation. As the team grows and
tackles more complex projects simultaneously, software and infrastructure challenges emerge.

I walk through building a data science platform step-by-step, from the ground up, starting with a
single data scientist and scaling to a full team handling multiple projects. By zeroing in on the
emerging pain points at each stage, the platform stays lean - addressing only what's genuinely
needed, and making every investment count. The result is a data science team that are enabled and
empowered in their applied-science work, and not bogged down by maintaining a complex platform,
nor held back by infrastructure that doesn't facilitate the needs of their projects.
