## MSA Pairing Transformer: protein interaction partner prediction with few-shot contrastive learning

**ICML 2024 Workshop on Efficient and Accessible Foundation Models for Biological Discovery**,
June 2024, dvp authors: Alex, Dan

We tackle protein sequence pairing within interacting protein families by fine-tuning the MSA
Transformer using contrastive learning applied to embeddings from scrambled single-chain multiple
sequence alignments. Our approach achieves high pairing accuracy even within small sets of pairable
sequences, outperforming prior co-evolutionary statistical methods. The paired cross-chain MSAs
demonstrate stronger encoding of interface contacts compared to established heuristic approaches,
suggesting potential for extending co-evolutionary analysis to protein-protein interactions.

**Keywords:** protein-folding, protein language model, protein-protein interaction, MSA,
contrastive learning
